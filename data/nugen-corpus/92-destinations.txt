# Curated destinations

100 destinations, each with a written description, the things it is
best for, and when to go. This is a different set from the local picks
catalogue: these are destinations with a photograph and a coordinate, not
individual recommendations.

## Edinburgh

- Id: `edinburgh`
- Region: Europe
- Country: Scotland
- Coordinates: 55.9533, -3.1892
- Best for: Old Town closes and closes, Whisky, Book shops, Hiking day trips
- When to go: May to September; the festivals are August, the lights are the first week of January
- Built on an extinct volcano and split by a ravine, Scotland's capital is all dark stone, sudden closes of light, and a literary habit of putting the view to work. The castle sits on an extinct volcano; the New Town below it was laid out in the 1760s and still reads as one coherent idea.

## London

- Id: `london`
- Region: Europe
- Country: United Kingdom
- Coordinates: 51.5072, -0.1275
- Best for: Neighbourhoods, not monuments, Markets, Free museums, Riverside walks
- When to go: April to June and September to October
- Two thousand years of settlement stacked on top of each other, and the point of a visit is not the list of sights but the fabric between them. Every bridge and market here has an argument about it, which is why asking a local beats reading a list.

## Dublin

- Id: `dublin`
- Region: Europe
- Country: Ireland
- Coordinates: 53.35, -6.2603
- Best for: Live trad music, Bookshops, Trinity and the Georgian squares, Coastal walks
- When to go: May to September
- A compact, walkable capital of Georgian squares and pub doors, on a bay that turns the whole city toward the water. Literary Dublin is real but the pubs are better: the city has one of the highest densities of live music in Europe.

## Killary Fjord

- Id: `killary-fjord`
- Region: Europe
- Country: Ireland
- Coordinates: 53.6167, -9.8
- Best for: Fjord cruises, Mountain cable cars, Total quiet, Killeen Rune Famine memorial
- When to go: May to September; it is a wet, changeable place year-round
- Ireland's deepest fjord, twenty minutes off the Ring of Kerry road and almost entirely undeveloped. The cable car up the mountain and the boat cruise into the fjord are the two reasons to leave the coastal road for it.

## Reykjavík

- Id: `reykjavik`
- Region: Europe
- Country: Iceland
- Coordinates: 64.1458, -21.9425
- Best for: Geothermal pools, Northern lights, Icelandic food, Whale watching
- When to go: June to August for midnight sun; September to March for the aurora
- The world's northernmost capital of a country, which is why everything in it is either geothermal, painted in corrugated iron, or both. From the harbour you can see the glaciers, and in winter the aurora is a genuine possibility rather than a brochure claim.

## Bruges

- Region: Europe
- Country: Belgium
- Coordinates: 51.2089, 3.2242
- Best for: Canals at dawn, Belgian chocolate and beer, Flemish painting, A walkable medieval core
- When to go: April to June and September to October; December has a famous Christmas market
- A medieval trading city barely larger than its own centre, where canals run between brick gables and the only serious traffic is bicycles. The high-impact car-free zone covers most of what visitors want to see.

## Amsterdam

- Id: `amsterdam`
- Region: Europe
- Country: Netherlands
- Coordinates: 52.3728, 4.8936
- Best for: Canal cycling, Rijksmuseum and Stedelijk, Brown cafés, Anne Frank House (book ahead)
- When to go: April to May and September; the flower fields around the city are April only
- A city built on piles, laid out in a semicircle of canals, and defended by being exactly flat and exactly open. More than 800 bridges and a houseboat culture that is a way of life rather than a tourist attraction.

## Paris

- Id: `paris`
- Region: Europe
- Country: France
- Coordinates: 48.8567, 2.3522
- Best for: Neighbourhood markets, Small museums, Canal Saint-Martin, Boulangeries
- When to go: May and September to October; the Parisians leave in August
- Everyone gets the Louvre. The better Paris is in the markets, the small museums, and the arrondissements one metro stop from where everyone else is not. The city is a set of 20 distinct neighbourhoods that happen to share a postcode.

## Route de Napoléon

- Id: `route-de-napoleon`
- Region: Europe
- Country: France
- Coordinates: 45.1234, 6.5872
- Best for: Road cycling, Alpine passes, Restorative history, Long-distance riding
- When to go: June to September, before the alpine snow returns
- A 260-kilometre road climbing from the Mediterranean to the Alps, planted with monuments in 1821 and kept passable for cyclists ever since. It is a cycling road before it is a pilgrimage, and the switchbacks on the Col de Vars are the honest part.

## Berlin

- Id: `berlin`
- Region: Europe
- Country: Germany
- Coordinates: 52.52, 13.405
- Best for: Street art and the remaining Wall, Museum Island, Kreuzberg and Neukölln food, Club and techno culture
- When to go: May to September; December for the markets and the lights
- A city assembled from other cities' rubble, which explains the density of walls, murals and unfinished buildings. Its culture operates on a 24-hour clock and treats the alternative scene as a mainstream industry rather than an undercurrent.

## Dessau

- Id: `dessau`
- Region: Europe
- Country: Germany
- Coordinates: 51.8333, 12.25
- Best for: Bauhaus architecture, Modernist housing still in use, Gropius and G furniture, A working design school
- When to go: April to October; the school tours run through the week
- The city Walter Gropius built in the 1920s to house the Bauhaus, and the clearest statement anywhere that design is a social programme rather than a look. It is a working pilot project on a grey river, not a museum set-piece.

## Prague

- Id: `prague`
- Region: Europe
- Country: Czechia
- Coordinates: 50.0875, 14.4214
- Best for: Beer halls, The Castle District at dawn, Vltava riverside, Cubist architecture
- When to go: April to June and September to October
- A city of spires and cellars, where the beer is cheaper than the water and the river is the only free view of the skyline. The Castle District sits above a medieval grid that survived four centuries largely because commerce was here, not monuments.

## Kraków

- Id: `krakow`
- Region: Europe
- Country: Poland
- Coordinates: 50.0778, 19.7847
- Best for: The medieval Main Square, Zapiekanka at Plac Nowy, Kazimierz after dark, Auschwitz, an hour away
- When to go: April to June and September to October; December for the markets
- Poland's former capital, and the only major medieval city in the country that was never destroyed, which is why its Old Town is intact rather than reconstructed. Kazimierz, the old Jewish quarter, has its own separate and harder history.

## Vienna

- Id: `vienna`
- Region: Europe
- Country: Austria
- Coordinates: 48.2083, 16.3725
- Best for: Café culture, Classical music, Naschmarkt, The Danube and the Schloss Belvedere
- When to go: April to June and September to December
- An imperial capital that went out of business gracefully, leaving coffee houses, wine cellars and 1,600 public buildings nobody had to demolish. The city is a UNESCO listing in its entirety, which sounds like hyperbole until you try to list what is excluded.

## Budapest

- Region: Europe
- Country: Hungary
- Coordinates: 47.4925, 19.0514
- Best for: Thermal baths, The Great Market Hall, Ruin bars beyond Szimpla, The Danube at night
- When to go: April to June and September to October
- Two cities on one river, Buda in the hills and Pest on the flat, joined by the Chain Bridge and thermal baths that the Romans started. Ruin bars, an enormous market hall, and a ruin pub scene with more honesty per square metre than most cities manage.

## Šibenik

- Best for: The stone-built old town, Cathedral of St James, Krka and Skradin day trips, Swimming off the stone quays

## Dubrovnik

- Id: `dubrovnik`
- Region: Europe
- Country: Croatia
- Coordinates: 42.6403, 18.1083
- Best for: Walking the city walls, King's Landing filming locations, The Lokrum island swim, Early morning before the crowds
- When to go: April to June and September to October; July and August are intense
- A walled city of terracotta roofs on a cliff, encircled by 1,940 metres of medieval fortifications. It is beautiful and it is managed: the old town's street furniture is regulated, cruise crowds arrive in waves, and the honest advice is to come early.

## Rome

- Country: Italy
- Coordinates: 41.8933, 12.4828
- Best for: The Forum at opening time, Testaccio market, Trastevere for dinner, Appian Way by bike

## The Veneto

- Coordinates: 45.7333, 11.85
- Best for: Palladian villas at Vicenza, Prosecco hills, Verona's arenas and markets, The Euganean Hills

## Aeolian Islands

- Id: `aeolian-islands`
- Region: Europe
- Country: Italy
- Coordinates: 38.5333, 14.9
- Best for: Black sand beaches, Volcanic hot springs, Stromboli at sunset, Complete quiet
- When to go: May to June and September to October; the ferries thin out in winter
- Seven volcanic islands north of Sicily, two of which are still erupting, and the only UNESCO site in Europe recognised for its geology rather than its architecture. Vulcano has the black sand beaches; Stromboli has the volcano you can walk up to at dusk.

## Athens

- Region: Europe
- Country: Greece
- Coordinates: 37.9842, 23.7281
- Best for: The Acropolis at opening time, The street food around Monastiraki, The rooftop tavernas, Cape Sounion at sunset

- A city built on seven hills with the Acropolis on the highest one, layered with Roman, Byzantine and Ottoman Athens under the modern capital. The best surviving ancient urban planning in Greece is in the streets around the Agora, not on the rock above it.

## Aegean Sea

- Region: Europe
- Country: Greece
- Coordinates: 39, 25
- Best for: Island hopping by ferry, Sailing and small-boat charters, Beaches after August, Diving and snorkelling

## Barcelona

- Coordinates: 41.3833, 2.1833
- Best for: The architecture of Cerdà, Gràcia and Poble-sec bars, La Boqueria and markets, The beach after dark

## Lisbon

- Coordinates: 38.7253, -9.15

## Porto

## Saint Cuthbert's Way

- Id: `saint-cuthbert-s-way`
- Region: Europe
- Country: England
- Coordinates: 55.6776, -1.5949
- Best for: Multi-day walking, Holy Island and Lindisfarne, The Farne Islands seabirds, The Cheviot Hills

## The Tweed

- Coordinates: 55.7652, -1.9909

## Cairo

## Rabat

## Cape Town

- Region: Africa
- Country: South Africa
- Coordinates: -33.9253, 18.4239
- Best for: Table Mountain on a clear day, Cape Point and the peninsula, Constantia wine tasting, Bo-Kaap and District Six
- When to go: October to April for weather; December to mid-January is peak price
- A city between a mountain and two oceans, which means you can be in a Table Mountain gallery and a surfing beach within the hour. It is expensive and it is unequal, and both facts shape what you should see and where.

## Victoria Falls

- Id: `victoria-falls`
- Region: Africa
- Country: Zimbabwe
- Coordinates: -17.9333, 25.8333
- Best for: The gorge and Devil's Pool, Sunset from the Zambian side, Airstrip safaris next door, The Zambezi upstream
- When to go: May to October; the falls are at full flow then and at their fiercest
- The largest sheet of falling water on earth, sixteen kilometres wide at its widest, and a UNESCO listing shared with neighbouring Mosi-oa-Tunya in Zambia. The Zimbabwean side gives the view; the Zambian side lets you walk on the Knife-Edge bridge into the spray.

## Gombe National Park

- Id: `gombe-national-park`
- Region: Africa
- Country: Tanzania
- Coordinates: -4.6667, 29.6333
- Best for: Wild chimpanzees, not a sanctuary, Lake Victoria sunsets, Forest research talks, Birding, with 300 species
- When to go: June to October and December to February; the rains close the trails
- The only place in the world with a guaranteed wild chimpanzee population, on the shore of Lake Victoria where the forest still meets the water. Research has run here since 1960, so habituation is genuine rather than staged.

## Tsavo National Parks

- Id: `tsavo-national-parks`
- Region: Africa
- Country: Kenya
- Coordinates: -2.7786, 38.7717
- Best for: Elephant herds at scale, The Shetani lava flows, Mombasa Gate from the highway, Arabuko-Sokoke forest, adjacent

## São Tomé

- Id: `sao-tome`
- Region: Africa
- Country: São Tomé & Príncipe
- Coordinates: 0.3375, 6.7283
- Best for: Community-run forest tours, Cacao and spice plantations, Pico Cão Grande, Almost no other visitors
- When to go: June to September and December to March; the wet season is short and green

## Istanbul

- Region: Middle East
- Country: Türkiye
- Coordinates: 41.0136, 28.955
- Best for: Bosphorus ferry crossings, The markets and street food, Hagia Sophia and the Cistern, The Princes' Islands ferries

## Petra

- Id: `petra`
- Region: Middle East
- Country: Jordan
- Coordinates: 30.3221, 35.4515
- Best for: The Treasury at first light, The Monastery, 800 steps up, The Siq approach, Wadi Rum directly afterwards
- When to go: October to April; summer midday heat makes the Siq punishing
- A Nabataean city carved into rose sandstone canyons, reached by walking 800 metres through a natural slot that is designed to be narrower than the Treasury it frames. Two thousand years of abandonment have preserved it because nobody could rebuild it.

## Wadi Rum

- Id: `wadi-rum`
- Region: Middle East
- Country: Jordan
- Coordinates: 29.5931, 35.42
- Best for: Red sand and clear skies, Bedouin camp with no light pollution, 4x4 and scrambling routes, Film locations in earnest
- When to go: March to May and September to November; cold nights, so bring a layer
- A 10,000-year-old red desert of sandstone mountains and dune fields, and the best-preserved film-scape on earth. The Bedouin camps are the point: tea by the fire, no Wi-Fi, and the clearest night sky in the Middle East.

## Oman

## Jaipur

- Id: `jaipur`
- Region: Asia
- Country: India
- Coordinates: 26.915, 75.82

## Taj Mahal

- Id: `taj-mahal`
- Region: Asia
- Country: India
- Coordinates: 27.175, 78.0419
- Best for: Sunrise from the east gate, The mosque and the guest house across the river, The marble inlay close up, Agra Fort nearby
- When to go: October to March; buy tickets well ahead and enter at sunrise
- A Mughal mausoleum built by an emperor for his wife over roughly twenty years, in white marble inlaid with semi-precious stone. It changes appearance entirely with the light and the season, from pink at dawn to gold at sunset, and the haze over the Yamuna can close the view for days.

## Varanasi

- Id: `varanasi`
- Region: Asia
- Country: India
- Coordinates: 25.3189, 83.0128
- Best for: Dawn on the river ghats, The Ganges Aarti at dusk, Sarnath and the Buddhist sites, Aligarh's silk weaving
- When to go: October to March; the crowds at peak pilgrimage months are extreme
- One of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world, on the Ganges, where the cremation ghats run continuously and the ritual is not a spectacle to be consumed but a life to be observed. The Ghatavasana on a new-moon morning is the largest human gathering on earth, and the city is at its most extraordinary.

## Ranthambore

- Region: Asia
- Country: India
- Coordinates: 26.0203, 76.455
- Best for: Wild tigers in forest, not a zoo, The Chittorgarh and Kumbhalgarh ruins in the park, Bagh Ahar, Peak-morning and peak-evening drives
- When to go: October to June; the park closes for the monsoon
- The most reliable place in India to see wild tigers, in a 1,000-square-kilometre national park of lakes, ruins and forest where the ruins date to the ninth century. Sightings are genuinely uncertain, which is the correct way to approach it.

## Goa

- Region: Asia
- Country: India
- Coordinates: 15.5, 73.83
- Best for: South Goa beaches, Spice plantation tours, Old Goa's churches, Dudhsagar Falls by train
- When to go: November to February; the monsoon breaks from June to September
- A state of beaches, Portuguese-era churches, and a hinterland of spice farms and the Western Ghats, which is the part most visitors never see. The south of the state is the quieter half, and the Dudhsagar Falls railway runs through the forest.

## Kerala Backwaters

- Id: `kerala-backwaters`
- Region: Asia
- Country: India
- Coordinates: 9.8635, 76.3594
- Best for: A dawn houseboat through the canals, Kumarakom bird sanctuary, Periyar wildlife sanctuary, Ayurvedic clinics in Kovalam
- When to go: November to March; the monsoon is heavy from June to August
- A 900-kilometre network of lakes and canals threaded through coconut and paddy, where a houseboat trip is the local form of a weekend. The canals run between Kumarakom and Alleppey, and the town of Alleppey is the centre of it.

## Darjeeling

- Id: `darjeeling`
- Region: Asia
- Country: India
- Coordinates: 27.0375, 88.2631
- Best for: The Toy Train on a UNESCO line, Singbulli's tea gardens, Tiger Hill for the Everest view, Kalimpong and Yuksom
- When to go: October to December for clear Himalayan views; March to May for rhododendrons
- A hill station in the eastern Himalaya where the air, the tea and the rail all date from the British Raj, and where the Toy Train still runs on a narrow gauge. Singbulli and Kalimpong are better than Darjeeling itself for an unhurried stay.

## Gujarat

- Id: `gujarat`
- Region: Asia
- Country: India
- Coordinates: 23.22, 72.655
- Best for: Bandhani and block printing in Kutch, Rani ki Vav stepwell, Modhera's Sun Temple, White Rann salt desert
- When to go: November to February; the Rann is walkable only in the cool months
- Western India's most distinctive state, and it is a textile state: block-printed, tie-dyed, embroidered, and mirror-worked cloth made in kutch and around Surat. Rani ki Vav in Patan is a stepwell built like a temple upside-down, and Modhera's Sun Temple predates Konark by five centuries.

## Bangkok

- Region: Asia
- Country: Thailand
- Coordinates: 13.7525, 100.4942
- Best for: Canal and river boats, Street food markets, Wat Pho and Wat Arun early, Talad Rot Fai and the Chatuchak weekend market

## Hanoi

- Id: `hanoi`
- Region: Asia
- Country: Vietnam
- Coordinates: 21, 105.85
- Best for: The Old Quarter's street food, Egg coffee and ca phe sua da, Hoan Kiem Lake at dawn, Water puppet theatre

## Danang

- Country: Vietnam
