# Catalogue taxonomy

The 890 places are filed under 8 categories. Counts
below are the real distribution, including the untagged bucket.

## Categories

- **Restaurants** (`restaurants`) — 86 places.
- **Shops & Markets** (`shopping`) — 112 places.
- **Sightseeing** (`sightseeing`) — 20 places.
- **Tours** (`tours`) — 34 places.
- **Ticketed Attractions** (`attractions`) — 31 places.
- **Nightlife** (`nightlife`) — 9 places.
- **Hotels** (`hotels`) — 26 places.
- **Unfiled** (`unfiled`) — 576 places.

## Budget bands

- **Budget** (`budget`) — 384 places.
- **Mid-range** (`mid-range`) — 203 places.
- **High-end** (`high-end`) — 21 places.
- **Price unknown** (`unknown`) — 282 places.

Budget is missing on 282 places, which is the largest single band.
That is an absence in the source, not a price of zero and not a judgement that
the place is cheap.

## Provenance

Every field in the catalogue is `curated`, `provider`, `osm` or `inferred`.
Nothing here was invented to fill a column. Where a value is missing it is
missing on the page too.
