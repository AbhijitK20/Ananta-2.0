# Quests

222 quests in four tiers. A quest pays its reward once, on claim, and its
goal is always a statement about what has been stamped — never about
distance, time or money.

## Tiers

- **Getting started** (`warmup`) — 9 quests.
- **City clearance** (`city`) — 200 quests.
- **Category hunts** (`category`) — 8 quests.
- **Long game** (`grand`) — 5 quests.

## Getting started

- **First Footfall** (`warmup-first-stamp`)
  Stamp your first place.
  Goal: stamp 1 places in total.
  Reward: 10 XP.

- **Getting Oriented** (`warmup-five`)
  Stamp five places, anywhere.
  Goal: stamp 5 places in total.
  Reward: 40 XP.

- **Well Rounded** (`warmup-variety`)
  Collect from four different categories.
  Goal: stamp places across 4 different categories.
  Reward: 60 XP.

- **Eat, Drink, Shop, See, Do** (`warmup-spread-five`)
  One place in each of five categories.
  Goal: stamp one place in each of 5 categories.
  Reward: 120 XP.

- **Widely Travelled** (`warmup-ten-cities`)
  Collect in ten different cities.
  Goal: stamp places in 10 different cities.
  Reward: 150 XP.

- **Unmarked Map** (`warmup-unfiled`)
  Stamp five places the dataset could not categorise.
  Goal: stamp 5 Unfiled places.
  Reward: 80 XP.

- **Cheap Eats, Real Places** (`budget-budget`)
  Stamp ten budget places.
  Goal: stamp 10 Budget places.
  Reward: 120 XP.

- **The Comfortable Middle** (`budget-mid-range`)
  Stamp ten mid-range places.
  Goal: stamp 10 Mid-range places.
  Reward: 120 XP.

- **Treating Yourself** (`budget-high-end`)
  Stamp five high-end places.
  Goal: stamp 5 High-end places.
  Reward: 120 XP.
