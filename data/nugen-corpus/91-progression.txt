# Progression

Only one thing is stored: the set of stamped place ids, and the day keys on
which a stamp happened. Everything else below is derived from that set at
read time, so none of it can disagree with the stamp book.

## XP

- Per stamp: **10 XP**.
- Clearing an entire city: **50 XP**.
- Completing the daily challenge: **15 XP**.

## Streaks

A streak is the run of consecutive days on which at least one place was
stamped. Stamping on consecutive days extends it; a day with no stamp ends
it. The current streak, the best streak and the days kept are all read off
the stored day keys.

## Achievements

15 achievements in three tiers. Tiers differ in lightness and
saturation as well as hue, so they stay distinguishable in greyscale.

- **Doorway** (`ach-first`, bronze) — Stamped the first place.
- **Getting the Hang of It** (`ach-ten`, bronze) — Ten places stamped.
- **Regular** (`ach-fifty`, silver) — Fifty places stamped.
- **Well Travelled** (`ach-two-hundred`, gold) — Two hundred places stamped.
- **Somewhere to Start** (`ach-first-city`, bronze) — Stamped a place in a new city.
- **Ten Cities** (`ach-ten-cities`, silver) — Collected in ten cities.
- **Continental** (`ach-thirty-cities`, gold) — Collected in thirty cities.
- **Eclectic** (`ach-five-categories`, silver) — Five categories collected in.
- **Full Inventory** (`ach-every-category`, gold) — One place in every category, unfiled included.
- **Three in a Row** (`ach-streak-three`, bronze) — A three-day collecting streak.
- **A Full Week** (`ach-streak-seven`, silver) — A seven-day collecting streak.
- **Habit Formed** (`ach-streak-thirty`, gold) — A thirty-day collecting streak.
- **Unmarked Map** (`ach-unfiled-ten`, silver) — Ten places the dataset could not categorise.
- **Middle of the Road** (`ach-mid-range`, bronze) — Fifteen mid-range places.
- **Treating Yourself** (`ach-high-end`, silver) — All five high-end places in the dataset.

## Stamps

A stamp is the only stored fact. There are 890 of them to collect,
across 202 cities and 8 categories. Stamps are keyed
`city/slug`, so a place is identified by its city and its slug together.
